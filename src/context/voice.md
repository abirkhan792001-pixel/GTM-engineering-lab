# Voice and copy guidelines

Template. Replace the examples with your own writing samples before generating live copy.

## Tone

- Plain, direct, peer-to-peer. Write like an operator talking to another operator.
- Confident, not salesy. Curious, not presumptuous.
- Phrase inferences as questions. Never present a guessed problem as a known fact.

## Hard constraints for email copy

- **Length:** first touch ≤ 90 words; follow-up ≤ 60 words. Subject line ≤ 6 words, lowercase is fine.
- **Sentences:** short. Aim for ≤ 15 words each; one idea per sentence.
- **One ask** per email. No multiple CTAs, no calendar link in a first touch.
- **One observation**, and it must come from the lead's `evidence`. No evidence means no personalised opener. Use a simple, role-relevant message instead.
- **No invented facts:** no fake case studies, customer names, metrics or "I noticed…" claims without a source.
- **No flattery:** avoid "love what you're building", "impressive growth", "big fan".
- **No false familiarity:** no "hope you're well", "just circling back", "quick question" unless it really is one.

## Banned buzzwords

synergy, leverage, game-changer, revolutionize, cutting-edge, best-in-class, seamless, robust, unlock, supercharge, empower, world-class, holistic, paradigm, disrupt, 10x

## Structure (first touch)

1. Observation grounded in evidence (1 sentence).
2. Why it matters to this persona's pain point (1–2 sentences).
3. What we offer, stated plainly (1 sentence).
4. One low-friction ask (1 sentence).

## Review checklist

- [ ] Every claim traces to `evidence` or the approved offer.
- [ ] No banned buzzwords.
- [ ] Merge variables rendered; nothing like `{{first_name}}` left in the text.
- [ ] Experiment metadata (`testRound`, `variantId`, `promptVersion`) attached to the draft.
